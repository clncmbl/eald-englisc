⌜sendon⌝  Above "sendon" a later scribe has glossed with "ferdon
ofer sæ".  That is the wording used in the MS E (The Peterborough
Chronicle).

⌜Brytwalas⌝

⌜bædon⌝  Past for biddan.  For the second instance
Manuscript A has "bædan" with an "o" written over the
"a" as a correction.  The verb biddan uses the accusative
for the person or other entity to which the request
is made and uses the genitive for the thing requested.
[[ So why "heom" (dative)? ]]

⌜Ætla⌝  Above "Ætla" a later scribe has glossed with "Ætilan in
lib. Petroburg" (at least, that's what jebbo claims it says; I
am unable to read the manuscript well enough to say).  MS E
(Peterborough) says "Ætlan".
