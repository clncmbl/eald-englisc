The lone warrior often awaits grace, the Creator's mercy,
though he had to anxiously stir the front-cold sea, travel
the exile-paths over the sea-way.  Fate is fully resolute.

Thus spoke the earth-stepper, mindful of hardships, of
wrathful slaughter, of the downfall of dear kinsmen:

"Often I had to bemoan my sorrow alone every predawn.
None is now of the living to whom I dare openly speak
my mind.  I truly know that in a warrior a noble virtue
is that he firmly bind his soul-enclosure, keep close his
hoard-chamber, think as he might.
